# Story pass: does the text blend with the picture? (art and atmosphere review)

Reviewed: commit 9d08ad1 "Tell the story" (`git show HEAD -- src/`), against `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`, `docs/design/LOCATIONS.md`, `docs/design/CHARACTERS.md` 1-143 and `docs/design/STORY.md`. I looked at the frames from a `route-art.mjs` run: a copy of `route.mjs` in the scratchpad that also screenshots each caption chunk and each exit glide. I ran it on the long route at 1024 (`shots/art-long-1024/`) and 375 (`shots/art-long-375/`), plus a short run that opens the office window (`shots/art-win-1024/`, `art-win-375/`). The stock `route.mjs long` gives up at the cold open ("waited 30 s on [SKIP INTRO]"), because the cold open now holds longer than its 30-second patience.

## Verdict

**APPROVE WITH CHANGES.** The pass does what the studio head asked for: the city, the missing man, Vale and what the batteries mean now reach the screen. Most new lines speak the art's plain, wet, amber-and-cyan register. The text strip never breaks at either width: two lines on desktop, three on phone, no chunk cut off, and no buttons crowded. The problems are timing and a few lines that describe things the frame does not show. First, the extra words stretch almost every entry hold. The cold open grows from 8.8 s to 37.2 s over a push that stops after 8 s, and the chase waits 25 s before the first prompt. Second, putting Rook's quote first pushes each set's description past the camera move it was written for. Third, four lines contradict the frame: the window lantern, the arm over the shoulder, the medic waiting, and "in there" inside the club. Fourth, three endings now name the lamps going out after the lamps have gone out. All the fixes below are text-only and can land without touching mechanics or cameras. The chapter and hit/miss cards are clean: no new caption hedges a result card or contradicts one. The clock and the light stay honest from 23:40 to first light.

## Findings

1. **BLOCKING. `officeEntry`, `00-officeEntry.png`, `-chunk1..3.png` (1024 and 375).** `"Halvard, 23:40, the ninth night of rain. ... his lamp is the last one burning on the floor."` The log shows **4 chunks, holds 9.7/10.3/8.5/8.3 = 37.2 s**, on a 6 s cutscene whose push settles at 8 s. That leaves about 29 s of a still office under exposition: a wall of text, not a slow crane. Every new player sees it first, and it is four times the old 8.8 s. Cut it to three chunks of about 27 s, and drop "at Night Division" (the block-letter card already says it). Replacement:
   `Halvard, 23:40, the ninth night of rain. The Lumen Board rations the power, and the towers uptown buy most of it. Lamplighters keep the low streets lit by hand, one battery to a lamp. Ivo Bell has been missing four nights, and his street is going dark. Missing persons is Rook's desk, and his lamp is the last one burning on the floor.`

2. **BLOCKING. `officeDesk` window, `art-win-1024/01-officeDesk-look1.png`.** `"Below the window Station Road is down to a few lamps, and one lantern is moving between them, lighting the rest."` The window shows only the four `cityRow`s dropped 6 units: facades and lit windows. There is no road, no street lamps and no moving lantern (`officeSet()` draws none). This is the one beat whose whole point is looking out, and it describes a picture that is not there. Replacement:
   `Through the blind the towers uptown burn all night; somewhere under them, Bell's street has gone dark. Rook takes his coat.`

3. **BLOCKING. `pump>roof` transition, `22-transit-pumpTruth-to-roofEntry-glide.png`, then `22-roofEntry.png`.** `"Up the service stair with Bell's arm over Rook's shoulder, out of the water and up to where a medic can reach him."` During the glide, Rook (cyan, left) and Bell (amber, right) stand apart on the walkway. The exit point is a ladder at z 31, not a stair. The roof then fades up with Bell already seated beside the medic while Rook walks in alone. The second half is also explanation, and it types over black. Replacement:
   `Up the service ladder behind Bell, rung by rung, toward the roof and a medic.`

4. **BLOCKING. `canalEnd` closings, `59-canalEnd.png`, `59-canalEnd-chunk1.png`.** The lamps go out from 4 s to 10 s of the 10-second crane (`scenes.js` line 522). The added meaning sentences push the lamps sentence to **12.1 s (board), 12.3 s (paper), 9.6 s (word) and 12.4 s (krane)**. It used to arrive at 7.2, 8.1 and 2.2 s. Now the case's last image plays under "in ink that stayed dry", and the line naming it types over lamps that are already out. Put the lamps second in each closing. The `paper` line also drops "Vale signed it, and this time somebody read it back" (see 12). Replacements:
   `Bell is alive. The lamps go dark along the canal, and this time it is only because morning has arrived. Vale is in custody, and the name above his is on the warrant, in ink that stayed dry. The station route gets its batteries back.`
   paper: `Bell is alive. The lamps go dark because it is morning. Vale is at large, but the paper is dry and it names the Board. The warrant has two names on it.`
   word: `Bell is alive. The lamps go out along the canal because the sun is up. Vale is in custody, and it is Bell's word against a Night Division inspector's. The name above Vale's is still a set of initials, and the batteries are still uptown.` Then, as now, `It will have to be enough.` or the notebook tail.
   krane: `Bell is alive. The lamps go dark along the canal; morning has come. Vale is gone, but his bodyguard is in a splint in the room next to Vale's office, and Krane has begun to talk. The man who turned the key on Bell is naming who paid for it.` The stalled variant gets the same reorder.

5. **SHOULD. `roofEntry`, `22-roofEntry-chunk1.png`, `-chunk2.png`, `-chunk3.png`.** `Rook: "Bell is breathing, and Vale does not know it yet, but that will not last the hour."` reads as if Bell will be dead within the hour. `"the towers uptown blazing"`: the frame is a dim field of far windows with a few amber and rose blocks, and nothing blazes. `"Ines Okafor ... is waiting by the roof radio with a blanket"`: the medic stands beside Bell, who is already seated at the radio, and no blanket is drawn. The fix also cuts four chunks (31.4 s) to two (18 s). Replacement:
   `Rook: "Bell is alive. Within the hour, Vale will know it." On the roof the city opens beneath them: far windows lit uptown, the low streets dark. Ines Okafor, a city medic, already has Bell sitting down by the roof radio.`

6. **SHOULD. `clubEntry`, `38-clubEntry-chunk1.png`.** `Rook: "Vale is in there, spending what Bell's lamps were worth. I go in alone, and every table is his."` The line types while the dolly is already inside the club, past the singer, with Vale's booth ahead. "In there" puts Rook outside a room the frame has him walking through. Replacement for the opening quote (the rest is unchanged):
   `Rook: "Vale is at the back, spending what Bell's lamps were worth, and every table between us is his."`

7. **SHOULD. `roof>tram` transition, `27-transit-roofConfession-to-tramEntry-glide.png`.** `"Vale's car went west, and the last tram of the night is going the same way."` The camera glides to the lift cage, but the line names no move. 8. **SHOULD. `tramEntry` and `chaseEntry`, `27-tramEntry-chunk2.png` and `43-chaseEntry-chunk2.png`.** Rook's quote now sits between the transition line and the establishing sentence, so the description arrives after the camera has settled. `"Rook drops from the service lift onto the last tram"` types at 9 s, when the camera has been riding low along the wire for seconds. `"The patrol car is in Vine Alley with the keys in"` types at 8.8 s over the elevated road at speed. The chase now holds 25.2 s before its first prompt, against the Act 2 grammar of 5 to 8 s entries. In moving sets, the picture sentence should come first and Rook's line second. STORY.md's rule should read "every entry caption *carries* one Rook line" rather than "opens with". Replacements:
   `Rook drops from the service lift onto the last tram; below, lamps 14 to 19 on Bell's route stand dark. Rook: "I left Bell on a roof for this." Dispatch will bring the patrol car to Market Arch.`
   `The patrol car is in Vine Alley with the keys in, as dispatch promised. Rook: "If Vale reaches the towers, nobody on our floor brings him back down." Vale's red tail lights race ahead into the elevated traffic.`

9. **SHOULD. `pumpFind`, `pumpTruth` and `roofConfession`, `16-pumpFind.png`, `21-pumpTruth.png`, `26-roofConfession.png`.** The new speeches run past 150 characters, so the chunker splits them mid-quote.
